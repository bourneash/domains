# Expand SKU registry from 24 → 40

Goal: 40 SKUs by month 1. Each SKU =
- 1 review page (`/reviews/<id>/`)
- +1 entry on its varietal page
- +1 entry on its vessel page
- +1 `/go/<id>` redirect (auto-generated by `build-redirects.mjs`)

## Candidate gaps (by form + varietal)

- **Black**: need another premium loose Darjeeling, a smoked Lapsang
  Souchong, a Keemun
- **Green**: need Sencha, Dragon Well (Long Jing), Genmaicha
- **Oolong**: Ti Kuan Yin (Iron Goddess), Da Hong Pao, Phoenix
- **White**: Bai Mu Dan (Peony), aged Shou Mei
- **Pu-erh**: a sheng (raw) cake to balance the shou (cooked) tuocha,
  one premium aged brick
- **Matcha**: an ultra-budget Aiya/Maeda en, a USDA-organic option
- **Herbal**: Pukka turmeric, a German chamomile-only tin
- **Vessels**: Yixing clay pot, a budget electric kettle (no temperature
  control), a thermal travel infuser flask, a gaiwan
- **Accessories**: tea timer, ceramic tea caddy set, microfiber tea
  towels, electric milk frother (for matcha lattes)
