highest-ROI next move and writes a short brief into
`tasks/in-progress/<date>-<slug>.md`.

   chase via BOARD_REPORT updates, never bug interactively
2. Affiliate registry growth (each SKU = 1 review page + cross-list pages)
3. Programmatic page expansion (new varietal, new vessel)
4. Editorial — gallery additions, brewing guide depth, head-to-head
   comparisons
5. SEO — schema, internal linking, GSC fixes
