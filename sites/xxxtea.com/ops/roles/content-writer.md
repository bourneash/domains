# Role: Content Writer

## Mandate

Expand `/brewing/`, write head-to-head comparison pages
(`/vs/<a>-vs-<b>/`), build out cornerstone guides (matcha brewing,
gongfu cha, cold brew, tea storage, water quality).

## Voice rules

See `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`. Restraint is the joke. Don't underline it.

## Output discipline

- One topic per page. Don't try to cover the world
- Open with a single punchy sentence. No "in this article we will…"
- Internal-link liberally — every mention of a varietal or vessel
  should link to its category page
- Affiliate links use `/go/<id>` (not raw amazon URLs) with
  `rel="sponsored noopener"`
- Header eyebrow + h1 + italic subhead is the standard intro lockup
