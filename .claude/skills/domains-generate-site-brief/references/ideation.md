# Ideation method (Mode B — no idea given yet)

Jesse doesn't want generic "here are some niche site ideas" listicles. He wants sites that fit the
fleet's proven shape: small, weird, niche, voice-driven, and — per the ultrarough proof point —
capable of real affiliate revenue precisely *because* they commit hard to a specific, strange angle
instead of playing it safe. Restraint and specificity are the joke; genericness is the failure mode.

## How to generate candidates

1. **Read the current fleet roster** (`DOMAINS_INDEX.md`) before proposing anything — don't pitch a
   niche that's a near-duplicate of an existing site's angle.
2. **Look for gaps in the niche graph**, not gaps in the market. The fleet doesn't compete on "is
   there search volume" alone — it competes on "is there a weird, specific, underserved angle nobody
   else is willing to commit to the way we will." Cross adjacent categories the way broadwayshowgirls
   or ultrarough do (persona + affiliate + a genre nobody else takes seriously).
3. **Voice-first, not product-first.** Start from "what's a specific flavor of weird/edgy/queer-coded
   we haven't used yet" and find the niche that flavor fits, as often as starting from the niche and
   bolting voice on after.
4. **Revenue-shape check.** For each candidate, name the plausible Amazon-affiliate category (real
   products that exist and convert) or ad-serving angle, or explicitly mark it non-revenue if it's a
   portfolio/community play. Don't hand-wave "we'll monetize somehow."

## Presenting candidates

One paragraph each, covering:
- The niche + the specific hook (not just "a site about X").
- Why it's underserved — what existing sites in the space get wrong or play too safe.
- Revenue shape (affiliate category, ad-serving, or non-revenue).
- The voice angle — what's this site's specific flavor of weird/edgy/queer, distinct from other fleet
  sites' flavors.

Let Jesse pick (or reject all and redirect). Once he picks, run Mode A on the winner using the
template in SKILL.md.

## Guardrails

- Never invent brand positioning as if it were settled — a candidate pitch is a *proposal*, not a
  brief. It only becomes a brief once Jesse picks it and you run it through Mode A.
- Don't over-index on ultrarough/xxxtea's specific sultry-restraint voice as the only flavor of
  "weird" — the fleet's queer-coded/edgy identity has room for other angles (absurdist, unhinged,
  deadpan-clinical-but-not, folk-horror, chronically-online). Vary it deliberately across new sites.
